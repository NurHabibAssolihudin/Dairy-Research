# PERBANDINGAN PASAR: INDONESIA VS US/EUROPE

## KARAKTERISTIK PASAR

### Indonesia
- Budget range: Rp 5-50 juta per project
- Decision maker: Pemilik bisnis langsung
- Sales cycle: 1-2 minggu
- Meeting preference: Face-to-face/WA
- Payment terms: DP 50%, cicilan umum
- Platform populer: Instagram, TikTok, WA

### US/Europe
- Budget range: $3,000-$15,000 per project
- Decision maker: Marketing team/committee
- Sales cycle: 3-4 minggu
- Meeting preference: Email/Zoom
- Payment terms: Milestone-based
- Platform populer: LinkedIn, Email, Instagram

2. Key Selling Points:
   - Quality & expertise

3. Communication Channels:
   - Email primary
   - Professional calls
   - Project management tools
